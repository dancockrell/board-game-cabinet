# Wooden set provenance

All six chess silhouettes in `symbols/` were authored specifically for this project as simple SVG geometry. They use the familiar visual vocabulary of chess (crossed king, crowned queen, crenellated rook, horse knight, split mitre bishop, round pawn). No external fonts, photographs, models, or asset packs are embedded.

Wood grain is generated by the project shader. Board, frame, chips, and selection markers are procedural Godot meshes. This first pass uses dark grain-modulated surface brands; it does not claim physically cut engraving geometry. The inset top disk and lip provide physical depth around each branded face.

Move audio is synthesized locally by `presentation/move_audio.gd` from damped oscillations and seeded noise. No recorded samples or external audio are used.
