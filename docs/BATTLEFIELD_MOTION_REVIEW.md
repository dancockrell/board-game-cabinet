# Native battlefield motion review

Use tools/review_battlefield_motion.gd with --motion-output=<absolute directory>. It runs the actual Olympus controller, performs legal card requests, warms the match to 30 seconds, then captures 180 presentation frames at 30 fps through second 36. It preserves the normal interface and board camera. The sidecar records time, legal deployment count and whether the displayed snapshot matches the session.

This is a six-second normal-speed playback sample, not a full-match or all-directions coverage claim. The warmup is omitted from the video. It supplements isolated pose studies and the complete accelerated match verifier. The first richer-pose capture is outputs/Battlefield-Motion-Richer-Poses.mp4; its sidecar confirms five legal deployments including warmup and matching authoritative state. Review frames 0, 90 and 179 together with playback, not a single attractive frame.

Current visible priorities beyond gait work: authored character defeat/reaction clips, richer attacks in the shorter directions, secondary body motion, and unit readability where formations overlap near bridges. The current unit departure is a dissolving copy of the last pose, not a drawn collapse animation. Building collapse already has authored sequential drawings. Do not confuse the two.
