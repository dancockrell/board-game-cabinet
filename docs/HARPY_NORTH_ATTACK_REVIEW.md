# Harpy north attack: bounded eight-pose study

The north attack remains the older four-entry sequence with three unique drawings. No new north attack was admitted in this pass.

Three built-in image-generation calls attempted a rear-facing eight-pose claw swoop: preparation, knee bend, tuck, initial projection, strike, follow-through, leg recovery and hovering recovery. The first source kept the character identity and produced useful preparation/recovery drawings, but its strike feet projected back toward the viewer. The second call explicitly corrected strike direction and mostly retained that error. The third requested small far-side talons at belt height; it removed the visible legs from almost every middle phase, including frames it was told to preserve. That sheet does not provide a readable complete strike.

All three unchanged originals, exact prompts and SHA256 hashes are preserved locally in the shared library under `assets/candidates_needing_review/olympus-harpy-north-attack-eight-2026-09-08/provenance.json`. They are candidate studies, not shipped assets, and were not pushed to the public shared repository. Existing runtime Resources were left unchanged. No native animation acceptance is claimed for these rejected sheets.

Next attempt should author one isolated north-facing strike with clear foreshortened leg anatomy, then build the adjacent poses from that accepted anatomy. Another whole-sheet correction risks changing otherwise usable preparation and recovery frames. The south-facing eight-pose attack remains separately admitted.
