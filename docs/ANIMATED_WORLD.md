# Animated world production direction

User direction, 2026-09-07: make twenty times the animations considered sufficient, with a minimum of about twenty animations per item; animate the whole world.

## Scale and counting

Working interpretation pending clarification: at least 20 distinct base clips per item, expanding toward 400 coherent action/direction/variation clips. Frames are not separate animations. The JSON catalogue is production planning, not generated inventory. Its 140 starter action names do not represent completed assets. Directional coverage and meaningful contextual variations can expand a base clip, but duplicates, retiming and renamed copies do not satisfy the target.

Every item needs its own coverage plan. The family lists are starting vocabulary, not a universal checklist to force onto every object. A rock can respond through moss, droplets, insects, surface light, weather and interaction; it need not wobble. A tree needs different rhythms from a soldier. Replace unsuitable generic actions with meaningful item-specific ones while preserving the requested richness. Do not add game mechanics merely because a visual action exists.

## Coherence before volume

Prove one Greek fighter and one small environment patch against the selected pixel references. First verify a complete cycle, consistent equipment, transparent extraction, scale and pivot. Expand to the minimum catalogue once the method works, then broaden directions and variants. This is a production order, not a reduction of the user's target.

Each clip has ordered frames and individual timings, a stable pivot, facing, loop/recovery behavior and named visual event markers. Generated sprite grids are candidates until extracted and played. True alpha is preferred; otherwise use a flat chroma color absent from the palette. Do not key a light background against ivory cloth or skin highlights. Keep shadows separate. Review against light, dark and actual terrain.

## A living world that remains readable

Units have breathing, anticipation, recovery and personality. Architecture has doors, banners, torches, occupants and visible reactions. Vegetation responds to wind, rain and contact. Water has currents, foam, ripples and splashes. Small props and effects follow their own material behavior.

Use long quiet holds, varied phase offsets and coordinated wind/weather events. Do not start every loop simultaneously. Avoid constant full-screen motion: battle cues need visual priority. Freeze offscreen animation work, use shared atlases where coherent, and schedule ambient motion less often at distance. Provide reduced ambient motion without hiding essential combat feedback.

The simulation remains authoritative. Presentation event markers synchronize visuals; they do not decide damage or resource spending. Cosmetic clip selection uses a separate deterministic seed and never consumes gameplay randomness.

## Production records

See ANIMATION_CATALOGUE.json for starter families and required clip metadata. Track planned, generated, rejected, accepted and integrated counts separately. Only accepted clips count toward delivered coverage. Preserve sources and provenance. No paid generation; Magnific browser generation only with visibly confirmed Generate Unlimited. Bulk volume does not authorize paid tools or unchecked submissions.

Current checkpoint is direction and catalogue only. No sprite animations have been generated or integrated by this change.
