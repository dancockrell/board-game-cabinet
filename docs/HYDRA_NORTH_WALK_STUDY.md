# Hydra north walking — eight-pose production study

2026-09-08. Built-in image generator only, three calls. Source originals and intermediate sheets preserved in shared local folder `assets/candidates_needing_review/olympus-hydra-north-eight-2026-09-08`; nothing pushed to the public shared repository.

Resource: `res://themes/hydra_north_walk.tres`. Eight unique 384×512 regions, row-major order, 0.92-second loop. Explicit upper-row pivot450 and lower-row pivot432 align ground contact. Use only for north/rear locomotion; coordinator owns board mapping.

The first generation hid the left foot under a broad tail and repeated the other foot. Targeted tail narrowing exposed both hind feet, followed by a phase correction establishing left lift/reach/contact and right lift/reach/contact. Three heads, green/gold palette and full rear identity remain consistent.

Native review captured60 frames and inspected all eight phase representatives over the actual painted battlefield. Dedicated native clip tests pass unique source cells, timing, loop, pause and reset. This is a staged actor review, not a full-match integration test.

Remaining defects: the high hind-foot lift reads as a deliberate heavy stomp and still benefits from finer intermediate poses; front-foot motion is mostly obscured; tail-tip sway and hip transitions remain coarse. These limitations are preferable to the old stationary breathing substitution, but this is not final polish.
